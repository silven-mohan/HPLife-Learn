### What is AI?:

#### Tasks that are performed by **AI**:
> * Recognizing Speech, Decision Support, Assistance, Analyzing Data, Driving Vehicles, Translation and many more....

#### ***Types of AI: Narrow AI vs. General AI

* **Narrow AI:**
	Also known as **Weak AI**. It is designed to perform specific task or set of tasks with high level proficiency. It is defined as "Narrow" due to the fact that it cannot learn, apply, create beyond it's programmed domain.

	Examples of Narrow AI: Voice Assistants like Alexa, Siri and recommendation algorithms on platforms like Netflix, Spotify,..


* **General AI:**
	Also known as **Strong AI**. This AI possesses the capability to understand, learn and apply it's knowledge over a wide range of tasks at a level comparable to human intelligence.

	No AI yet reached this level of intelligence. And to achieve this level of AI is still an ongoing research...


### The Role Data in AI:
	Data serves as the foundation of AI providing the raw material on which the models learn, make predictions and generate insights.

     * The Quality of the data provided is directly linked to the AI performance.

#### Importance of Data in AI:
	**Big Data** plays an important role in training sophisticated AI models, with large and diverse datasets enhancing models capabilities..

	* What is **Big Data**:
		The large and complex datasets produced at high velocity gathered from various sources. These datasets are difficult to analyze using traditional tools but provide better 	insights when analyzed by AI.

	* Various methods of Data Collection: **Surveys**, **Web Scraping**, **Sensors(devices that were used for measuring a physical quantity)**, **Other Types like: A/B testing, APIs, 		Crowdsourcing, .....**

##### Data Preprocessing:
	This ensures clean, structured data used for training AI models for better accuracy and performance.
	Techniques of Preprocessing: Normalization, Standardization, Handling missing and incomplete data, scaling and transformation.

### Machine Learning:
	Basically we make AI learn a specific task or a wide range of tasks by providing the Big Data without explicitly programming each task so that it can recognize patterns and many more. This is an iterative process.
	
	* SUPERVISED LEARNING: 
		So in this process we provide **labelled data** that is the both input and corresponding correct output is provided while training.
		Example: House price predictions: Labelled data like inputs: House location value, size and correct outputs for that corresponding input data.

	* UNSUPERVISED LEARNING:
		So in this process we provide only **unlabelled data** that is the inputs by which the predicts the outputs based on the pattern recognitions.

### Deep Learning:
	In Deep Learning neural networks contains interconnected layers(many) with nodes to model complex patterns and representations. The inputs are passed to nodes and then those inputs are processed and passed to subsequent nodes resulting in output.

	Example: Generative AI

	* A key layer in Generative AI is Large Language Models(LLM) which work on Deep Learning.
